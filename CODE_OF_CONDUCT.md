# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful and assume good faith. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal
attacks, harassment and demeaning comments are not.

Concretely, in this project that means:

- Critique the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Assume the other person knows something you do not, especially about a platform
  or a vendor you have not used.
- Keep discussion in the open where possible, so decisions can be found later.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a private security
advisory or by email. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit,
remove or reject contributions that violate this document, and may block repeat
offenders.

This document is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), kept short on
purpose.
